## Admission batch complete

IDs 11727–11736 were inserted and hardware-captured as 61 states. Their
focused generator uses only `bytearray(512)` raw little-endian memory images;
it contains f64 memory load/store, word/qword FILD/FISTP, ordered/equal/greater/
unordered and empty-stack FUCOM/FUCOMI states, MMX MOVQ loads/stores, and
full-stack FLD overflow states.

IDs 11737–11770 add 160 captured states: f80 extended load/store, non-pop f64
and integer stores, FCOM/FUCOM pop and integer-flag forms, four FCMOV
conditions, x87 control/status load-store forms, and 18 core MMX arithmetic,
logic, comparison, packing, and unpacking forms. IDs 11771–11803 add 118 more:
x87 transcendental and constant pushes, BCD conversion, FISTTP word/qword, and
MMX variable shifts, multiply, average, sum-absolute-difference, and masked
store. IDs 11804–11832 finish every x87 mnemonic family supported in long mode:
integer arithmetic/compare, reverse/pop arithmetic and compare, FFREEP, wait
and no-wait control, and waiting legacy environment/status saves. The obsolete
287-only FNENI/FNDISI/FSETPM families are deliberately excluded from this
long-mode corpus. Every x87 input has physical `x87_r0`…`x87_r7`, a physical
abridged `x87_tag`, TOP 0/3, distinct raw payload slots, and the required
zero/normal/denormal/infinity/qNaN/unsupported/empty vectors. IDs 11860–11891
then add the remaining scalar operand forms: word integer arithmetic/compare,
f32/f64 arithmetic and compare/compare-pop, f32 store, dword integer store,
and reverse register-destination forms. MMX rows include matching `mm0`/`mm1`
views.
